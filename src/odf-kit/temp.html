Alphabet pronunciation


A: a

B: bé

C: cé | normally k but c when before i,e,y

D: dé

E: e

F: effe

G: gé | normally g but j when before i,e,y

H: hache | silent

I: i

J: ji

K: ka

L: elle | there is no dark L in french.

M: emme

N: enne

O: o

P: pé

Q: ku | always paired with a silent u with a few exceptions (cinq).

R: erre | normally غ, but it's خ if it's after:

k, q, ch (k sound)

t (t sound)

p

f, ph (f sound)

ch (sh sound)

s (very rare)

S: esse } normally s, but z when between 2 vowels (and not ss).

T: té

U: u

V: vé

W: double vé | only in borrowed words, pronounced as W in english words and as V in german words.

X: ics

Y: i grec | i, but if between 2 vowels or in the verb payer it's i+i.

Z: zède

Compound consonants



ph: f

gn: ny

ch: sh, The CH makes a K sound almost exclusively in words of Greek origin.

qu: k

th: t

LL: Sounds like a normal L after A, E, O, U (balle = bahl). But after the letter I, it turns into a Y sound (fille = feey).
